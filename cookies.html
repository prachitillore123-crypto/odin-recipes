Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

This recipe makes the ultimate chocolate chip cookies: golden brown and crispy on the outside,but soft,thick,and perfectly chewy on the inside . Packed with rich chocolate chips, they are an absolute crowd-pleaser.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter(softened)
  • 3/4 cup white sugar
  • 3/4 cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups semi-sweet chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Preheat Oven:Preheat your oven to 375 degree F and a line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Cream Butter and Sugars:In a large bowl , beat the softened butter, white sugar , and brown sugar together until the mixture is smooth and creamy.
  3. Add Wet Ingredients:Beat in the egg one at a time ,then stir in the vanilla extract.
  4. Combine Dry Ingredients:Gradually mix in the flour, baking soda, and salt until a dough forms .Gently fold in the chocolate chips using a spatula.
  5. Scoop and Bake:Drop rounded tablespoons of dough onto the prepared baking sheet,leaving about 2 inches of space between each cookie. Bake for 9 to 11 minutes until the edges are golden brown.
  6. Cool:Let them cool on the baking sheet for 2 minutes before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely.
