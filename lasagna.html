World's Best Lasagna

This is a classic, rich ,and comforting italian lasagna recipe made with layers of savory meat sauce ,tender pasta sheets,and a creamy,melted three-cheese blend.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 1b sweet italian sausage or ground beef
  • 1 container (15 oz) ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg (beaten)
  • 4 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 jar (24 oz) marinara sauce
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat and Prep:Preheat your oven to heat 375degree F . In a bowl ,mix the ricotta cheese ,beaten egg,and a pinch of salt together.
  2. Brown the Meat: In a large skillet over medium-high heat,cook and brown the ground meat until fully cooked,then drain the excess fat and mix in the marinara sauce.
  3. Layer the lasagna:In a 9x13 inch baking dish, spread a thin layer of meat sauce at the bottom. Arrange 3 or 4 lasagna noodles over the sauce. spread a portion of the ricotta cheese mixture,sprinkle mozzarella, and top with more meat sauce. Repeat these layers until all ingredients are used,ending with a heavy layer of mozzarella and parmesan cheese on top.
  4. Bake: Cover the baking dish with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for an additional 15 minutes until the cheese is bubbling and golden brown.
  5. Rest and Serve:Let the lasagna cool and rest for 15 minutes before slicing so the layers hold together cleanly.
