Fluffy Homemade Pancakes

This classic recipe creates thick ,light ,and incredibly fluffy pancakes from scratch.They are perfect for a cozy weekend breakfast and taste amazing topped with butter and warm maple syrup.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 tablespoon white sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tablespoons butter(melted)

Steps

  1. Mix Dry Ingredients:In a large bowl ,sift together the flour ,baking powder ,sugar , and salt. Make a well in the center of the mixture.
  2. Add Wet Ingredients:Pour the milk, beaten egg , and melted butter into the well . Whisk everything together gently until the batter is smooth. It is completely fine if there are a few small lumps left!
  3. Heat the Pan:Heat a lightly oiled griddle or frying pan over medium-high heat . Pour or scoop the batter onto the griddle , using approximately 1/4 cup for each pancake.
  4. Flip:Cook until bubbles pop on the surface of the pancake and the edges look dry ,the flip and cook the other side until it turns golden brown.
  5. Serve:Serve immediately while hot with your favorite toppings.
